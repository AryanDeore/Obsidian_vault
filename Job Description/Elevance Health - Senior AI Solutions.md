**Senior AI Solutions Engineer (Engineer Senior)**

**Location:** This role requires associates to be in-office 1-2 days per week, fostering collaboration and connectivity, while providing flexibility to support productivity and work-life balance. This approach combines structured office engagement with the autonomy of virtual work, promoting a dynamic and adaptable workplace. Ideal candidates will be able to report to our Pulse Point location in Atlanta, GA. Alternate locations may be considered if candidates reside within a commuting distance from an office.

Please note that per our policy on hybrid/virtual work, candidates not within a reasonable commuting distance from the posting location(s) will not be considered for employment, unless an accommodation is granted as required by law.

The **Senior AI Solutions Engineer (Engineer Senior)** will build and scale intelligent systems that improve healthcare outcomes. You will design, build, and deploy machine learning and LLM-based systems, taking models from experimentation to production within assigned client group, business unit or corporate department and utilizing various customer technology platforms. Also responsible for delivery of end-to-end application, system development and maintenance on complex multi technology platforms within assigned client group, business unit or corporate department and utilizing various customer technology platforms.

**How you will make an impact:** 

- Maintains active relationships with product, data, engineering, architecture, and business teams to translate business problems into scalable AI/ML solutions.
    
- Applies AI/ML capabilities and modern AI tools to solve meaningful business problems, improve healthcare outcomes, and enable practical adoption across business workflows, digital products, and enterprise applications.
    
- Owns the change request process and may coordinate with other teams as necessary.
    
- Provides technical advice and weighs in on technical decisions that impact cross-functional teams, including AI/ML and advanced analytics solutions.
    
- Researches and proposes new technologies, keeping abreast of developments in AI and applying them to enterprise use cases where relevant.
    
- Designs, builds, and deploys end-to-end ML and LLM-based systems (data pipelines, models, prompts, APIs, and monitoring).
    
- Develops, evaluates, deploys, and monitors machine learning models for predictive, classification, optimization, personalization, and decision-support use cases.
    
- Builds applied AI solutions such as semantic search, RAG pipelines, and embedding-based systems.
    
- Develops production-ready AI/ML services, APIs, pipelines, and reusable components; improves performance, scalability, reliability, usability, and operational readiness in production.
    
- Performs technical design reviews and code reviews; provides technical leadership through solution design, best practices, troubleshooting, and mentoring junior team members.
    
- Assesses status and supports data information planning.
    
- Coordinates on-call support and ensures effective monitoring of systems, including AI/ML model performance and reliability.
    
- Mentors others and may lead multiple or small to medium-sized projects; influences engineering standards and technical direction.
    
- Identifies and proposes strategies around technical problems affecting the team, communicates standards, and gains buy-in on solutions.
    

**Minimum Requirements:** 

Requires a BA/BS degree in Information Technology, Computer Science or related field of study and a minimum of 5 years related experience; experience working with multi-dimensional platforms, and business and technical applications also required; or any combination of education and experience, which would provide an equivalent background.

**Preferred Skills, Capabilities and Experiences:** 

- Masters or PhD preferred.
    
- Advanced Python skills for machine learning and data processing preferred. 
    
- Experience across the full ML lifecycle (development, deployment and monitoring) preferred.
    
- Hands-on experience with LLMs, embeddings, RAG, or modern NLP techniques using vector search technologies like OpenSearch preferred.
    
- Experience building production AI systems (APIs, pipelines, microservices) preferred.
    
- Experience designing evaluation frameworks for ML/LLM systems preferred.
    
- Familiarity with responsible AI principles (fairness, explainability, privacy) preferred.
    
- Experience with cloud-based ML platforms and scalable architectures (AWS Bedrock, or equivalent AI model cloud providers) preferred.
    
- Healthcare domain experience, including familiarity with data privacy and regulatory requirements preferred.
    
- Multi database and/or multi language highly preferred.
    
- Experience mentoring others, leading multiple small projects and providing troubleshooting support highly preferred.

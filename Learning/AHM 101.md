##### What is AHM
We are a ACA broker. We partner with agents, we give them leads, we help process payments and they sell policies.

##### Pings → Leads
- **pings are converted to leads**
- People dial an ad for insurance. An agency picks up the call record.
- **Pings** are callers that we get from an agency that AHM can bid on.
- A **lead** record is created whenever we encounter a new person. Selling them a policy is unrelated. 
- Pings are callers we can bid on. We hope those callers are new leads but we won't know until we talk to them.
